2. Run it on your PC

Put index.html in a folder, open a terminal in that folder, and run:
bash

python -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0

Find your PC's LAN IP address:

    Windows: ipconfig

    Linux: hostname -I

For example, if your PC IP is 192.168.1.50, open this on your phone:

http://192.168.1.50:8000

Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi and allow port 8000 through your PC firewall if necessary.
